How do I interpret user needs and implement them into a program? How does creating “user stories” help with this?
I interpret user needs by trying to understan what function the user is trying to accomplish, rather than focus on the specific feture that thy are asking for. Creating user stories help explain the need from a user's point of view, structered as "as a user, I want to do x, so that I can get y". What does it mean to be a good team member in software development?
Being a good team member in software development means to accuratly complete your part of the work, communicate effectively, and support your peers. It also means asking questions when you need clarification and clearly understanding what is expected. Another important part is being open and accepting to feedback, and being open to changes throughout the development process.
